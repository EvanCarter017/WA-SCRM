## Why this matters

The owner, 2026-11-22: "Why does the list automatically scroll back to the top every time I select a customer from the bottom of the list?"

Choosing a conversation draws the whole chat screen again, and the list of conversations is drawn with it — from its top. With 87 conversations, the employee who scrolls down and opens a customer loses their place in the list every time and no longer sees which conversation is selected. Reproduced in a browser before this plan was written: the list scrolled to its end (596 px), the last conversation chosen, the list back at 0.

What this plan does: where the list stands is noted whenever it is scrolled, and given back after every redraw, for as long as the list shows the same choice of conversations (the same number, filter and search). So the list stays put when a conversation is chosen, a message is sent or arrives, the employee comes back from another page, or — on a narrow screen, where an open conversation covers the list — goes back to the list. Another number, another filter or a search shows its list from the top.

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

- The place is noted from the list's own scroll events, not read when the page is redrawn: on a narrow screen the list is out of sight (and reads as "at the top") while a conversation is open.
- The code sits in the chat module's wrapper around `render`, the one place every full redraw goes through (it already keeps a playing voice note or video alive across a redraw).
- The feed's own refresh (`openwaRefresh`) keeps its line that gives the list its place back; it now does so a second time with the same value, which changes nothing.
- The texts, names and numbers in this plan are final.
