## Why this matters

On the WhatsApp API page (`#inbox`), the strip above an open conversation starts with two labels: the customer's follow-up status (for example `跟进已逾期`) and, when a to-do exists, `下次跟进` with its date. Today they are plain text. The product owner marked both labels on a screenshot on 2026-11-17 and said:

> "Clicking here should navigate to the Follow Up page and track this event."

Asked what "track this event" should do, the owner chose this option (and declined recording the click in the activity log):

> "The Follow Up page opens directly on this customer's follow-up item, with that row highlighted, so the agent can act on it at once. Mateo, who has an overdue follow-up, would land on his task in the to-do list. A customer with no scheduled follow-up would land on their row in the 'needs attention' list. A customer whose follow-up has ended would land on their row in the 'ended' list."

After this plan both labels are buttons. A click opens the follow-up centre (`#followups`) on the right tab, with the customer's row highlighted and scrolled into view.

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

1. "Here" is exactly the two labels at the start of the strip above an open conversation: the follow-up status label (in every state, including `跟进正常`) and the `下次跟进` label. Both do the same thing. Nothing else in the strip changes.
2. Where the click lands:
   - follow-up ended → tab `已结束跟进`, the customer's row;
   - otherwise, the customer has an unfinished to-do in the viewer's own queue → tab `跟进待办队列`, filter `全部待处理`, on the page that contains the customer's earliest unfinished to-do, that to-do's row;
   - otherwise → tab `待处理信号`, filter `全部信号`, the customer's row.
3. If the tag filter of the follow-up centre would hide this customer, the tag filter is cleared by the click.
4. The row stays highlighted until the user leaves the follow-up centre. No timer and no animation.
5. No activity-log entry is written for the click.
6. The labels keep their look. As buttons they get no border, the pointer cursor the baseline gives every button, an underline on hover and the tooltip `在跟进闭环中查看此客户`.
7. No new icon and no new colour value; the highlight uses existing colour tokens.
8. The version string stays `0.8-scrm-engagement`.
