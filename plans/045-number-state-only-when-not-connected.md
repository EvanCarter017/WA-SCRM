## Why this matters

At the top of an open conversation a line shows the employee's own number and, after it, the state of that number: a green dot and "在线" (online) while it is connected. The owner, 2026-11-22: "Is the 'online' status of customers accurate? Can we also display statuses like 'typing...'?" — the word was read as the customer being online.

What was found: the word is about the employee's number and is correct for it. A customer's "online" or "typing…" cannot be shown: the engine the number runs on (whatsapp-web.js) gets no such information from WhatsApp — OpenWA 0.31.0, the newest version, answers 501 to `presence/subscribe` and says so in its own list of capabilities; only its other engine (Baileys) can, which cannot fetch the history of a chat and needs the number to be linked again. Asked how to proceed, the owner chose to stay with the engine. Told that the word can only ever describe the own number and asked what to do with it, the owner chose "Hide it while connected": nothing is shown when the number is connected; a warning still appears in the same place when the number is disconnected and messages cannot be sent.

What this plan does: the line at the top of a conversation shows the number alone while it is connected. When the number has dropped, has to be scanned again or is not linked yet, its state follows the number as before (warning colour, `role="status"`).

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

- Only this line changes. The list of numbers, the choice of numbers above the list of conversations, the dashboard and the dialogs keep naming the state of a number — they are about numbers.
- The dot before the number's name in a row of the list of conversations stays as it is; the owner was asked about the word at the top of a conversation.
- The texts, names and numbers in this plan are final.
