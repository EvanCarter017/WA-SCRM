## Why this matters

In the `订单追踪` tab of the customer profile drawer every order is a block that always shows all of its shipping entries, so a customer with several orders fills the tab, and the current status of an order can only be read from its last entry. The product owner asked on 2026-11-18:

> "Regarding orders: Could they be made collapsible, with their status displayed via a tag or a segmented progress bar?"

Asked to choose, the owner picked a segmented progress bar (three segments for pending shipment → shipped → delivered, filled up to the current step, the filled part red on an exception, the status name on hover) and orders collapsed to one line by default (number, time placed, status and the icons; a click on the order number opens the block to show its entries and a second click closes it; the icons keep working on a collapsed order and do not open or close it).

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

1. Every order block is collapsed by default. Collapsed, it shows only its head; open, it also shows its entries, as today.
2. The order number in the head is a button (with an arrow icon in front: `ChevronRight` when collapsed, `ChevronDown` when open) that opens or closes the block. Its tooltip is `展开物流记录` or `收起物流记录`, and it carries `aria-expanded`.
3. Which blocks are open is view state only: a list of order ids in `lcState.orderOpen`, kept while the page is open, not saved, emptied by the demo reset. A new order starts collapsed. Opening one block does not close another.
4. The step, undo and remove icons work the same on a collapsed block and never change whether it is open.
5. After a click on the order number the keyboard focus returns to that button, because the drawer is re-rendered.
6. The head shows a progress bar of three segments after the time placed. The number of filled segments is the furthest step of `待发货 → 已发货 → 已签收` the order has reached: 1, 2 or 3. An order in exception keeps the segments it had reached. The filled segments take the colour of the current status — the same colours as the status labels of the entries: amber for `待发货`, blue for `已发货`, green for `已签收`, red for `异常`. The bar's tooltip is the status name.
7. The head is one row at every width and for every order: the title area (order number, time placed, progress bar) takes the free width and wraps inside itself when it is too long; the four icons stay at the top right and never drop to a second line. This replaces the wrapping head of plan 012.
8. The leading clipboard icon of the block heading is replaced by the arrow of the toggle button.
9. Existing colour tokens only; the icons `ChevronRight` and `ChevronDown` are already in `icons.js`. No new icon, no new colour value.
10. The version string stays `0.8-scrm-engagement`.

## Maintenance notes

- `lcState.orderOpen` is a list of order ids. It is touched in three places: the initial state, the demo reset and `lcOrderToggle`. Ids of removed orders may stay in the list; they match nothing and do no harm.
- The progress bar is derived on every render from the order's history: the furthest step of `LC_SHIPPING_PATH` among the entries. `problem` is not on the path, so an order in exception keeps the segments of the steps it had reached; the colour comes from the current status. Adding a step to the path means adding it to `LC_SHIPPING`, `LC_SHIPPING_NEXT` and `LC_SHIPPING_PATH`.
- The head's layout no longer depends on how long the texts are: the title area is flexible and wraps inside itself, the icon area has a fixed place. This also holds when a browser translates the texts.
- The toggle puts the focus back on its button after the drawer is re-rendered. The other actions of the drawer do not restore focus; if that is ever wanted, this is the pattern.
- What a reviewer should scrutinise: no action other than `lcOrderToggle` changes `lcState.orderOpen`; nothing about open or collapsed is written to an order or to `localStorage`; the entries of an open block are unchanged; the four icons keep their place; `icons.js` is byte-identical.
