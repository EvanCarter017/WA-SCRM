## Why this matters

The owner asked to "remove the 'Session Permissions' found on the right side of the session". In the conversation, the tool rail on the right ends with a shield button labelled 权限说明 ("permissions explained"); it opens a dialog titled 会话权限 ("conversation permissions", which a browser translates as "Session Permissions") that shows one fixed sentence — for a supervisor 主管可查看团队账号及其会话。, for an employee 聊手只查看分配给自己的账号及客户。 It explains, it does nothing; the rules of who sees what live elsewhere and stay as they are.

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

- The button goes from both definitions of the tool rail: the one that is drawn (`toolsRailHTML` in `fidelity-lifecycle.js`, which replaces the older one) and the older one in `fidelity-ui.js`, so that it cannot come back if the order of the modules changes.
- The dialog's action `fidChatPermissions` goes with it (nothing else opens it).
- The rail keeps its other six buttons in their order: 会话筛选, 客户 360° 档案, 安排客户跟进, 客户标签, 客户沟通备注, 刷新视图. The icon `ShieldCheck` stays in the icon set — the send guard and the account pages use it.
