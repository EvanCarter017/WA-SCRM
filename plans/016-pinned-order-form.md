## Why this matters

In the `订单追踪` tab of the customer profile drawer the form `登记订单` is the last block of the scrolling content, below the order blocks. Its place on screen therefore changes whenever an order is added, opened or closed, and with a long list it is scrolled out of view. The product owner outlined the form on a screenshot on 2026-11-18 and said:

> "Look at the area I've outlined: instead of having the "Register Order" section behave as a dynamic element, try keeping it fixed at the very bottom of the drawer at all times."

After this plan the form sits in a footer of the drawer that is always at the bottom edge; the first-contact block and the order blocks scroll above it.

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

1. On the `订单追踪` tab the drawer gets a footer below its scrolling body. The footer holds the form `登记订单`, unchanged in content and behaviour. The other tabs have no footer.
2. The footer is always at the bottom edge of the drawer, whatever the number of orders and whether they are open; the body above it scrolls on its own.
3. Pinning the form makes the scrolling area smaller, so two things that would otherwise get in the way are handled in the same change:
   - **Scroll position.** Every action in the drawer re-renders it, which today throws the scrolling body back to the top. When the same tab is re-rendered the body keeps its scroll position; when the tab changes it starts at the top. For the same reason the order toggle puts the focus back on its button without letting the browser scroll to it.
   - **Toasts.** Toasts of the drawer appear at its bottom edge, exactly where the footer now is, and a toast with an undo link stays until the next action. In the drawer a toast is placed above the footer, with the same 20 px distance it has from the bottom edge today. The drawer records the footer's height in a CSS custom property each time it is rendered.
4. The footer is a plain container with a top border; the form keeps its card look inside it. Existing colour tokens only; no new icon, no new colour value.
5. The version string stays `0.8-scrm-engagement`.
