## Why this matters

The owner manages follow-ups entirely by hand and asked: "Can't these records be removed manually?" They cannot: a to-do of the follow-up centre can only be finished with "完成并闭环", which asks for a result and for a next step or the end of the follow-up. A to-do made by mistake — or the one the old automatic rule left in the owner's workspace — can only be got rid of by pretending it was done. Asked, the owner decided on 2026-11-22: **add a delete button**.

What this plan does: every row of the queue of to-dos — pending or finished — gets a delete button (a bin icon). A click asks "删除待办「…」？删除后不能恢复。"; "确 定" removes the to-do, "取 消" changes nothing. After a removal the customer's next-follow-up time is that of the earliest to-do left (or none), and the customer's history gets the line "删除待办「…」".

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

- A confirmation, no undo.
- The same rule as for finishing a to-do: one's own to-dos (a manager: any).
- Deleting a to-do neither ends nor resumes the customer's follow-up, and removes nothing else.
- Only in the queue of the follow-up centre; the customer's file gets no such button in this plan.
