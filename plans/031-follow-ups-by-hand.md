## Why this matters

The owner saw a follow-up to-do in the workbench that nobody had made and asked: "are Follow-ups generated automatically? Shouldn't this be a scheduled task created manually?" Asked to choose, the owner decided on 2026-11-22: **manual only** — a follow-up exists only when someone creates it with a time.

Today the page makes a to-do by itself in two places:

1. when an employee makes a customer by hand: "首次主动触达 … 确认需求", due four hours later;
2. when a new person writes for the first time: "首响回复 … 并确认咨询需求", due two hours later, high priority.

This plan removes both. The two ways of making a to-do by hand stay exactly as they are: the form "新建跟进任务" (customer, what to do, when, priority) and the next step an employee enters when finishing a follow-up.

What still happens by itself, and is not a to-do: a customer with unread messages shows under the attention signal "客户待回复"; an open customer without a planned to-do shows "跟进断档". The customer's history keeps one line, "客户首次来信", for a first message.

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

- Manual only (owner, 2026-11-22). No setting to turn the automatic to-dos back on.
- To-dos that were made automatically before this change stay as they are; they are finished or ended like any other.
- After making a customer by hand the page says "客户 … 已创建，尚未安排跟进。"; it does not open the follow-up form by itself.
- The internal flag `needsFirstReply` keeps its name; it now only leads to the history line.
