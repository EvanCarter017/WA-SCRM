## Why this matters

Every row of the list of conversations ends with a line that names the number the conversation belongs to (or, when one number is chosen, the customer's language), and before it a dot: green while that number is connected, amber when it has dropped, grey when it is not linked yet. A green dot in the row of a customer reads as "this customer is online" — which WhatsApp does not tell this number (see plan 045, where the word "在线" at the top of a conversation was hidden for the same reason).

After plan 045 the owner was told: "The conversation list shows a green dot before your number's name on each row; it also only means your number is connected. Hide it while connected as well? Yes or no." The owner, 2026-11-22: "Yes, hide the green dot too".

What this plan does: the row draws the dot only when the number is not connected — amber when it has dropped or has to be scanned again, grey when it is not linked yet — in the same place as before. A connected number shows its name (or the customer's language) without a dot.

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

- The dot stays where a number is not connected: it is the warning that messages cannot be sent in that conversation.
- Nothing else in the row changes; the list of numbers, the dashboard and the dialogs keep naming the state of a number.
- The texts, names and numbers in this plan are final.
