## Why this matters

On 2026-11-24 the workbench's AI moved to Google Gemini. Reply translations on Gemini 3.5 Flash often took about 20 seconds; the owner asked "The translation latency is quite high—are you using an advanced reasoning model?", had the advisor try "Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Low)", and chose "Hydrogen at Low". Through the relay of this repository, Hydrogen at its default reasoning took about 3 s for a customer message into Chinese and 6–7 s for a reply with its context into Spanish; at reasoning Low about 1.3–3 s and 3–5 s. The relay can name the model in its settings file, but not how much the model reasons. With this plan it can: `AI_REASONING_EFFORT` in the settings file is sent to the model service as `reasoning_effort` (the field the OpenAI-compatible interfaces — OpenAI's and Gemini's — take for this).

### Decisions already made (do not re-open; do not ask)

The owner chose Gemini 4 Hydrogen at reasoning Low.

Decided by the advisor, within that:

- The setting is optional. Its values: `none`, `minimal`, `low`, `medium`, `high` (any case). Without it nothing is sent, so every server whose settings do not name it behaves exactly as before.
- A value outside these leaves the model service **not configured**, with the reason `bad-reasoning-effort` shown by the status (and so on the AI page), rather than being ignored: a mistyped setting is seen at once.
- The status names the effort when one is set (`reasoning`).
- It applies to every text request of the relay (translations and replies); speech to text is not concerned.
- After the deployment the reviewer adds `AI_REASONING_EFFORT=low` to the server's settings file (configuration, outside this plan's files).
